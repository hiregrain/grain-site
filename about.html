Then, one day, I was meditating under a tree, and I came to a profound realization of how to make a lot of money: Make a fantastically better version of Upwork! So, for the past year, my cofounder Kyle and I went deep into the talent industry. We met with…

KC

Hey, I'm Kyle.

I'm pretty obsessed with finding ridiculously good people and figuring out what they're actually great at.

Before Grain, I worked at MeritFirst, where I helped companies like Ramp, Corgi, Eragon, Clay, and Loop hire based on demonstrated ability instead of whatever AI-optimized stuff someone could cram onto a resume.

The thing I kept running into was that great people are everywhere, but hiring them at exactly the moment you need them is incredibly hard. Sometimes the perfect person doesn't want a full-time job. Sometimes you only need their expertise for a few weeks. Sometimes you just have an important project sitting around because you don't know who you can trust to own it.

That's what we're building Grain for.

You tell us what you need done. We find exceptional people who have actually proven they can do it, put the right team around the problem, and help you get the project across the finish line.

The best person for the job doesn't always need to become your next employee. Sometimes you just need to build with them.
