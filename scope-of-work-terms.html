Scope of Work Terms

Version 1.0, 2 September 2026. Every scope of work Grain sends incorporates these terms. Paying the invoice attached to a scope of work accepts that version of the scope and these terms.

1. Who and what

Grain Network Inc, a Delaware corporation ("Grain"), will deliver the work described in the scope of work these terms are attached to ("the Scope") to the customer named in it ("you"), for the fixed price stated in it. Grain sells a completed result, not hours. Grain decides who does each piece of the work, using its own people, contractors it engages, and software, and stays responsible to you for the result.

2. How the Scope is accepted

The Scope is accepted, and this agreement is formed, when you pay the invoice attached to it. The version you paid is the version that binds. Grain records the version, the link you paid through, and the time of payment as the record of acceptance. A later version replaces the earlier one only when you pay that version's invoice. If you reply to the Scope with changes instead of paying, Grain sends a new version. There is no other acceptance step.

3. What is in and what is out

The Scope lists what Grain will deliver, what it will not, and the open questions that must be answered before work starts. Work not listed as in scope is not included. A request outside the Scope is quoted as a new scope of work with its own price. Nothing outside the Scope is added to an invoice.

4. Your part

You will provide the inputs the Scope names, answer Grain's questions, and name one person who signs off the delivery. The clock does not pause. If an input or answer does not arrive, Grain works around it where it can and delivers what is complete on the promised date, with a note of what could not be done and why.

5. Turnaround

The turnaround in the Scope runs from the day Grain records your payment and counts calendar days, with no pauses. Grain tells you before the promised date if it will be missed and why.

6. Delivery, approval, and amendments

Grain delivers the work on the channel the Scope names, with a note of what is included. Within three calendar days of delivery you tell Grain one of three things: approved; approved with changes, naming which deliverables need amendment and which line of the Scope they miss; or rejected, naming the line of the Scope that is unmet.

  • Approved closes the project.
  • Approved with changes reopens the named deliverables. Changes inside the Scope are amended at no charge, within the amendment rounds the Scope states, which is two unless the Scope says otherwise. Changes outside the Scope are a new scope of work.
  • Rejected triggers the guarantee in section 7.
  • No answer within three calendar days of delivery counts as approved. Grain sends a reminder inside the window and records the deemed approval with its basis.

7. Satisfaction guarantee

If a deliverable does not meet the Scope's stated criteria after the amendment rounds, Grain refunds the price of that deliverable, or of the whole project if the Scope prices it as one, through Stripe to your original payment method. The full guarantee at hiregrain.com/guarantee forms part of these terms. Refunded work may not be used, and any rights in it under section 8 do not pass.

8. Ownership and process records

On payment in full, Grain assigns to you all rights in the deliverables made for you under the Scope, and warrants that it holds those rights from everyone who worked on them. Grain keeps its background methods, tools, templates, and know-how, and may use the general knowledge gained from the work.

Grain records how the work was done: which options were considered for each piece, who or what did it, what it cost, how it scored, and what you said about it. Grain may use these process records, de-identified and combined with records from other customers, to improve how it routes and checks work. Grain does not use the content of your deliverables or your confidential inputs to train models or to serve other customers without your written permission. Paying the invoice grants the process-record use described in this section.

9. Confidentiality

Each party keeps the other's non-public information confidential, uses it only for the project, and shares it only with people who need it to do the work and are bound to the same standard. Grain's contractors see only what their piece of the work needs. This section survives the project for three years.

10. Grain's people

Grain is an independent contractor to you, not an employee, partner, or agent. The people Grain engages are Grain's contractors, not yours; Grain is their counterparty and pays them. For twelve months after the project you will not engage any person Grain introduced to you through the project directly or through another intermediary without Grain's written consent.

11. Payment

The price is due in full before work starts, through the invoice attached to the Scope. You agree not to reverse or dispute a charge with your card issuer for a matter the guarantee in section 7 covers; the guarantee is the remedy, and Grain honours it through Stripe.

12. Warranty, liability, and indemnity

Grain warrants that the work will be performed in a professional and workmanlike manner and will meet the Scope's stated criteria. Beyond that warranty and the guarantee, the work is provided as is. Outcomes that depend on third parties or on your own actions after delivery are not guaranteed unless the Scope defines and prices them.

Neither party is liable to the other for indirect, consequential, or lost-profit damages. Grain's total liability under a scope of work is limited to the price paid for it.

Grain will defend and indemnify you against a third-party claim that a deliverable, as delivered, infringes that party's intellectual property, except where the claim arises from material you provided or from changes you made. You will defend and indemnify Grain against a third-party claim arising from material you provided or from your use of the deliverables outside the Scope.

13. Ending early

You may end a project before delivery by written notice. Grain delivers what is complete, refunds the price of any piece not started, and keeps payment for pieces started or complete. Grain may end a project on the same basis if you breach these terms.

Neither party is in breach for a delay caused by events outside its reasonable control, and the affected party tells the other promptly and resumes when it can.

14. General

These terms and the Scope are the whole agreement for the project and replace anything said or written before them. They are governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. Each party submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Delaware and waives trial by jury. If a term is unenforceable the rest stands. Notices go to the email addresses on the Scope and count as received when sent. Grain may update these terms for future scopes of work; the version in force when you paid governs your project.
