What this Privacy Policy covers

This Privacy Policy covers how we treat Personal Data that we gather when you access or use our Services. "Personal Data" means any information that identifies or relates to a particular individual, including information referred to as "personally identifiable information" or "personal information" under applicable data privacy laws, rules, or regulations.

This Privacy Policy does not cover the practices of companies we do not own or control or people we do not manage.

Personal Data we collect

We may collect the following categories of Personal Data, depending on how you use our Services:

Contact and Identity Data. Your name, phone number, email address, and the company you represent. You choose which of your phone number and email address to give us, and we require at least one so that we can reply to you.

Request Data. The description of the work you want done. When you submit the intake form, that description and your contact details are transmitted to Grain and stored in our systems, where the members of our team who handle incoming requests review them. We also collect any additional detail you provide when we follow up with you.

Project Data. If a project follows, the materials you share with us so that we can perform the work, the messages we exchange about it, the deliverables we produce, and records of your approval, rejection, or refund under the Grain Guarantee. The materials you share are yours, and we handle them as described in the Scope of Work Terms.

Payment Data. Records of the scopes of work you have accepted, the invoices issued to you, and whether and when they were paid. Payments are processed by Stripe, Inc. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

Communication Data. Text messages and emails we send you through our notification infrastructure (powered by Twilio, Inc. and Resend, Inc.), your messaging preferences and opt-out status, and any communications you send to us directly.

Device and Usage Data. Information about your device and browser, including IP address, IP-based location data, device type and operating system, and web page interactions. This data is collected automatically when you use our Services.

How we collect Personal Data

We collect Personal Data from the following categories of sources:

Directly from you, when you submit a request through the intake form, correspond with us about it, accept a scope of work, or share materials for a project.

Automatically, through server logs and session data generated when you use the Services.

From third parties, including publicly available business information we consult in order to scope and price a request, and the contractors and suppliers we engage to perform part of a project.

How we use Personal Data

We use Personal Data for the following purposes:

Providing and improving the Services. Reading your request; scoping and pricing it; preparing and sending you a scope of work; performing the work if you accept; engaging and supervising the contractors and suppliers who carry out part of it; administering the Grain Guarantee; providing support; conducting internal analytics; and performing fraud prevention, security monitoring, and debugging.

Communications. Sending service messages by SMS and email, including questions about your request, the scope of work itself, invoices, and updates while a project is running; responding to your correspondence; and communicating changes to our Services or terms. SMS message frequency varies, message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP to opt out of non-essential texts or HELP for help. We use your phone number for these messages and for confirming your identity when you contact us. We do not sell it or use it for third-party marketing.

Legal compliance and safety. Complying with applicable laws, regulations, or court orders; detecting and investigating security incidents or illegal activity; protecting the rights, property, or safety of our users or the public; and enforcing our agreements.

We will not use your Personal Data for materially different, unrelated, or incompatible purposes without providing notice or obtaining your consent.

How we disclose Personal Data

Mobile Information. We do not sell, rent, lease, or share your mobile phone number or your text-messaging opt-in or consent data with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile number is used only to deliver the messages described in this Policy and to confirm your identity, and is shared only with the SMS provider (Twilio, Inc.) that delivers those messages on our behalf. SMS message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply.

Service Providers. Third parties that help us operate the Services, including hosting and infrastructure providers (Vercel, Inc., Render, Inc., and Cloudflare, Inc.), SMS and email delivery providers (Twilio, Inc. and Resend, Inc.), and our payment processor (Stripe, Inc.). Each receives only the Personal Data it needs to perform its function for us and is not permitted to use it for its own purposes.

Contractors and Suppliers. To perform a project we engage contractors, and in some cases we buy goods or services on your behalf. A contractor receives only the Personal Data needed for the part of the project they are performing, and is bound by confidentiality obligations to us. Where a purchase must be made or delivered in your name, we share the details required to complete it.

Legal and Regulatory Authorities. We may disclose Personal Data when required by law, regulation, or court process; to prevent or investigate illegal activity; or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or the public.

Business Transfers. All Personal Data we collect may be transferred to a third party in connection with a merger, acquisition, bankruptcy, or other transaction in which that party assumes control of our business.

De-identified and Aggregated Data. We may create aggregated or de-identified data from the Personal Data we collect and share it for lawful business purposes in a manner that does not identify you personally.

Data retention

We retain Personal Data for as long as necessary to provide our Services or to perform our business or commercial purposes for collecting it. When establishing a retention period for specific categories of data, we consider who we collected the data from, our need for it, why we collected it, and its sensitivity. In some cases we retain Personal Data longer if doing so is necessary to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or collect fees owed. We may retain aggregated or de-identified data indefinitely.

We retain the details of a request until you ask us to delete them, so that we recognize you if you return to us later. We retain project records for as long as the Grain Guarantee on that project and our tax and accounting obligations require, and afterward only in the de-identified form described in the Scope of Work Terms. You can ask us to delete the details of a request at any time and we will do so, unless a project record must be retained.

Cookies

The Services use cookies and similar technologies such as pixel tags and web beacons (collectively, "Cookies") to enable our servers to recognize your browser, to keep the Services working, and to protect them against abuse.

Essential Cookies are required to provide core functionality you have requested, including submitting a request and maintaining the security of that submission. Disabling these may make parts of the Services unavailable.

We do not currently use advertising Cookies, and we do not permit third parties to place Cookies through the Services for advertising or cross-site tracking. If we add analytics, we will update this Policy to name the provider, and the site will ask for your agreement before setting any non-essential Cookie.

You can manage or disable Cookies through your browser settings. For more information, visit allaboutcookies.org.

Data security

We use appropriate physical, technical, organizational, and administrative security measures to protect your Personal Data from unauthorized access, use, and disclosure. Access to the requests and project records we hold is limited to the Grain personnel who need it to do their work, and those systems require authentication. You can help protect your data by limiting access to your device and by keeping sensitive material out of the intake form, which is not the right place for payment card numbers, credentials, or health information. No method of transmitting or storing data over the internet is completely secure.

Grain's Services are intended for businesses and for individuals 18 years of age or older acting on their behalf. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected Personal Data from a minor, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a minor has submitted Personal Data to us, please contact us at the address below.
