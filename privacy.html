Privacy Notice

Version 1.0, effective 2 September 2026.

What we collect

When you send a request through hiregrain.com we collect what you type: your name, work email, phone number if you give it, company, a target budget if you set one, and your description of the work. If a project follows, we collect what is needed to do it: the inputs you share, the messages we exchange, and payment records, which Stripe handles. We never see your card number.

We run no advertising trackers. If we add analytics to the site, this notice will name the provider and the site will ask before setting any non-essential cookie.

Why we collect it

To read your request, scope it, send you a scope of work, and do the work if you accept. Where a legal basis is required, ours is our legitimate interest in answering a business inquiry you sent us. We send marketing only if you asked for it, and every marketing email has an unsubscribe link.

Who sees it

People at Grain who work on your request. Contractors we engage see only the parts of a project they are doing, under confidentiality. Providers that process data for us: our hosting provider, our email provider, our file storage provider, and Stripe for payment. We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Request details until you ask us to delete them, so a returning customer is recognised however long it has been. Project records for as long as the project's guarantee and our tax and accounting obligations require, and afterward in de-identified form as the Scope of Work Terms describe. You can ask us to delete your request details at any time and we will, unless a project record has to be kept.

Your choices

Email hello@hiregrain.com to see what we hold about you, correct it, or ask for deletion. We answer within thirty days. We honour browser "do not track" signals by running no trackers in the first place.

Changes

We update the version and date at the top when this notice changes and note material changes on this page.
